    VisionCrowdTestCrowd Counting Benchmark for Vision-Language Models

    1,304 images398K+ annotated heads

    VisionCrowdTest is a diagnostic benchmark that asks whether the crowd counts produced by vision-language models are actually supported by spatial evidence in the image. It contains 1,304 images with more than 398K annotated heads, covering RGB, aerial, thermal and infrared imagery across low, medium and high crowd densities. Five recent VLMs are evaluated zero-shot on counting and spatial grounding, revealing a semantic-localization gap: plausible counts that correspond only weakly to where people actually are.

  • Recognition2026

    GenSyn10Multi-Generator Synthetic Image Dataset

    60,000 images3 generators

    GenSyn10 is a synthetic counterpart to CIFAR-10: 60,000 images at 32×32 across the same ten classes, with the standard 50k/10k train/test split. The images were rendered from grammar-based prompts by three architecturally different text-to-image models (FLUX.2-dev, HunyuanImage-3.0 and Qwen-Image-2512), making it a controlled benchmark for detecting AI-generated images and for studying generalisation across generators. Seventeen classification models are evaluated under a four-stage protocol: real-data baseline, zero-shot transfer, fine-tuning and retention.

    DermCaseRare Skin Disease Diagnostic Reasoning Benchmark

    6,354 rare cases26,030 image–caption pairs

    DermCase is a long-context dermatology benchmark for testing whether large vision-language models can reason their way to a diagnosis of rare skin diseases. Built from publicly licensed, peer-reviewed case reports, it holds 6,354 rare cases with complete diagnostic reasoning chains and 26,030 dermatological image–caption pairs. It supports evaluation of diagnostic accuracy, differential diagnosis and multi-step clinical reasoning, and 22 LVLMs are benchmarked on it.

    DRDDDiverse Road Damage Dataset

    1,500 images5 damage types

    DRDD targets road scenes in which several kinds of damage appear in the same frame, with about three damages per image on average. Its 1,500 images (1920×1440) were extracted from GoPro footage recorded in Oman under varying weather and times of day, checked by a civil engineer, and annotated with bounding boxes in Pascal VOC and COCO formats. Five classes are covered (alligator, block, longitudinal, pothole and transverse cracks), with occlusions, shadows and motion blur adding to the difficulty.

    PENDULUMAssessing Sycophancy in MLLMs

    751 images6 domains

    Dataset contains 751 images, each paired with approximately 2,000 visual question–answer (VQA) examples. The images span six distinct domains, each presenting different visual and reasoning challenges. For each question, three prompt types are included: a baseline (equilibrium) prompt with no user influence, a positive-influence prompt that provides a correct hint aligned with the visual evidence, and a negative-influence prompt that is misleading or adversarial.

    IARDIntruder Activity Recognition Dataset

    69,000+ frames4 classes

    IARD contains four classes — Armed Intruder, Door Kick, Intruder Inside, and Lock Breaking. Together, these classes span more than 69,000 frames. The dataset was built from carefully selected YouTube videos, cropped and manually annotated into the target activity classes, with standardized 224×224 frames. IARD was built with strict ethical safeguards: all videos were sourced from public sources, faces were anonymized, no identifying metadata was retained, and any sensitive or inappropriate content was removed. The dataset follows responsible AI and privacy principles.

  • Recognition2024

    UAV-AWIDUAVs in Adverse Weather and Image Distortions

    3 distortion types3 severity levels

    UAV-AWID is a set of test benchmarks for measuring how drone detectors cope with degraded images: rain at three intensities (drizzle, heavy, torrential), and motion blur and artificial noise at three levels each (low, medium, high). It is released together with the Complex Background Dataset (CBD) and an augmented version (ACBD) for training. Benchmarks of YOLOv5, YOLOv8, YOLO-NAS, Faster R-CNN and RetinaNet show mAP falling by up to 77 points under severe conditions, and training on the augmented data substantially improves robustness.

    CBDComplex Background Dataset for UAV Detection

    12,492 imagesUAVs & birds

    CBD supports drone detection against cluttered scenes. It brings together 12,492 images drawn from several public UAV datasets, spanning urban, semi-urban, indoor, outdoor and mountain scenes in sunny and cloudy conditions, with birds included as distractors and annotations provided in YOLO and JSON formats. The same paper introduces the Sky Background Dataset (SBD, 3,888 sky-only images) and a Rainy Test Set (RTS), which adds synthetic drizzle, heavy and torrential rain to 2,528 test images to measure how detectors degrade in bad weather.

    HICRDHeron Island Coral Reef Dataset

    11,676 imagesReal coral reef

    Heron Island Coral Reef Dataset (HICRD) contains 6003 low-quality images, 3673 good-quality images, and 2000 restored images. We use low-quality images and restored images as the unpaired training set (trainA + trainB). In contrast, the paired training set contains good-quality (trainA_paired) images and corresponding restored images (trainB_paired). The test set contains 300 good-quality images (testA) as well as 300 paired restored images (testB) as ground truth. All images are in 1842 x 980 resolution. The copyright belongs to CSIRO (Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation).

    UWSDUnderWater Synthetic Dataset

    10 water typesSynthetic underwater

    To synthesize underwater image degradation datasets, we use the attenuation coefficients described in Table 1 for the different water types of oceanic and coastal classes (i.e., I, IA, IB, II, and III for open ocean waters, and 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9 for coastal waters). Type-I is the clearest and Type-III is the most turbid open ocean water. Similarly, for coastal waters, Type-1 is the clearest and Type-9 is the most turbid. We apply Eqs (1) and (2) (please check the paper) to build ten types of underwater image datasets by using the RGB-D NYU-v2 indoor dataset which consists of 1449 images. To improve the quality of datasets, we crop the original size (480x640) of NYU-v2 to 460x620. This dataset is for non-commercial use only. The size of each dataset is 1.2GB.
