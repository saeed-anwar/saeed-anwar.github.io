Welcome

About me

I am a Senior Lecturer (equivalent to US Associate Professor) at The University of Western Australia, where I lead the Visual Intelligence and Analytical Imaging Lab. I am also a Visiting Scientist at CSIRO and an Honorary Faculty Member at the Australian National University.

  • Underwater imaging

Academia

I am a Senior Lecturer at The University of Western Australia and an Honorary Faculty Member at the Australian National University. Previously, I was an Assistant Professor at KFUPM, an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the University of Canberra and a Visiting Fellow at the University of Technology Sydney. Earlier, I held academic posts at NUCES and GIKI in Pakistan.

Industry

I am a Visiting Scientist at CSIRO, where I was previously a Research Scientist at Data61, and I was a Fellow at the SDAIA's Joint Research Center for AI. I also worked on research projects with National ICT Australia (NICTA), on a medical project with Toshiba Medical Visualization Systems Europe in Scotland, and on simulating sonar sensors for transparent object detection at PAL Robotics in Barcelona.
