Welcome

About me

I am a Senior Lecturer (equivalent to US Associate Professor) at The University of Western Australia, where I lead the Visual Intelligence and Analytical Imaging Lab. I am also a Visiting Scientist at CSIRO and an Honorary Faculty Member at the Australian National University.

  • Computer vision
  • 3D vision
  • Image restoration
  • Generative AI
  • Multimodal AI
  • Underwater imaging

Interested in a PhD, Master’s or Honours project? Find out how to join us.

Academia

Before joining The University of Western Australia in 2025, I was an Assistant Professor at KFUPM, an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the University of Canberra and a Visiting Fellow at the University of Technology Sydney. Earlier, I held academic posts at NUCES and GIKI in Pakistan.

Industry

I was a Visiting Scientist at CSIRO, previously a Research Scientist at Data61, and a Fellow at SDAIA's Joint Research Centre for AI. I also worked on research projects with National ICT Australia (NICTA), on a medical project with Toshiba Medical Visualization Systems Europe in Scotland, and on simulating sonar sensors for transparent object detection at PAL Robotics in Barcelona.
