PhD students

I’m looking for highly motivated doctoral students. Strong applicants usually have:

  • a high GPA
  • research experience, ideally with publications at leading journals or conferences
  • strong programming skills
  • excellent written and spoken English

There are three ways to fund a PhD with me:

Domestic scholarship
OpenDeadline 31 October
Open to Australian citizens and permanent residents, and to current UWA students.
Self-funded
OpenAll year
If you already have funding, such as a government scholarship, employer sponsorship or personal funds, you can contact me at any time of year.
International scholarship
ClosedOpens July to August
Applications open each year from July to August. You’ll need to meet UWA’s eligibility, English-language and visa requirements.

Visiting researchers

I host visiting researchers who bring their own funding; I can’t fund visits myself. If you have secured external funding and have a strong research background, please get in touch.

Master’s projects

If you’re a Master’s student at UWA or ANU looking for an individual research project, you should have a good GPA, some research background, solid programming skills and enough time in your schedule to commit to research.

Honours projects

I supervise two to three UWA Honours students each year and prefer 24-unit projects.

Research fellows

My group has no openings for research fellows at the moment. Any new positions will be listed on this page.

How to apply

  1. Check the openings table above and the requirements for your position.
  2. Email me with the position and your name in the subject line, for example “PhD enquiry: Jane Smith”.
  3. Attach your CV and academic transcripts, and include a short summary of your research interests with links to any publications or code.
  4. If there’s a good fit, we’ll arrange a call to discuss next steps.
