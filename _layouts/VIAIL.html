Research lab

VIAIL

Visual Intelligence and Analytical Imaging Lab

Directed by Saeed Anwar at The University of Western Australia, we research computer vision, visual informatics and AI-based image processing and analysis.

6Research themes 3Visitors

Our mission

Where vision meets intelligence

VIAIL is a hub of academic and experimental work at the intersection of two fields.

Visual informatics

Computer vision, data visualisation and information processing: turning images and visual data into understanding.

Analytical imaging

AI and machine-learning methods for image processing and analysis: restoring, enhancing and interpreting what cameras capture.

Our name

A name that sees, and speaks

A name that sees

VWeIEyeAIArtificial intelligenceLLab

Read aloud, the letters V, I and AI become “we”, “eye” and “AI”: we see the world through artificial intelligence.

A name that speaks

وئیل

In Pashto, viail means “to say” or “to express”. It captures our purpose: we speak through vision and images.

What we work on

Research themes

  • Computer vision

    Recognition, detection and scene understanding in images and video.

  • 3D vision

    Point clouds, depth estimation and LiDAR-based localisation.

  • Image restoration

    Denoising, deblurring, dehazing, super-resolution and low-light enhancement.

  • Generative AI

    Diffusion and generative models for images and 3D scenes.

  • Multimodal AI

    Vision–language models, visual storytelling and model reliability.

  • Underwater imaging

    Underwater image enhancement and marine scene analysis.
